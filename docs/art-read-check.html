Streetwise: Nicole & Stella — art review

Art Read-Check

Every sprite in the game — the obstacle roster, the pickups, and the cast — drawn from its exact procedural pixel data, blown up on an asphalt stage, and where it moves, playing the real animation. Obstacle cards carry a legibility verdict and a decision status; the one still-open call keeps a Now / Proposed comparison. Figures under each canvas are the drawn texture size, then the in-game height. Obstacles aren't tinted by the day/night cycle, so these colours are what you see in play.
