Streetwise: Nicole & Stella — art review

Art Read-Check

Every sprite in the game — the obstacles, birds, power-ups and the cast — drawn from its exact procedural pixel data, blown up on an asphalt stage, and where it moves, playing the real animation. Obstacle cards carry a legibility verdict and a decision status; the one still-open call keeps a Now / Proposed comparison. Figures under each canvas are the drawn texture size, then the in-game height. Obstacles aren't tinted by the day/night cycle, so these colours are what you see in play.

Daytime obstacles

Sunny-neighbourhood set — full weight until dusk (dayPhase 0.67).

Nighttime obstacles

Derelict-city set — hands off from dusk, all-night by dayPhase 1.

Birds

Not grounded. The pigeon and seagull reward a power-jump strike (a small boost and a full refill); the crow is hostile and dive-bombs Nicole — Stella intercepts the first dive of a run.

Power-ups

Floating collectibles with a timed effect: sneakers boost jumps (8 s), the zoomies treat sends Stella sprinting through small hazards and toppling carts (6.5 s), the pink star makes Nicole invulnerable (7 s), coffee slows the world (5 s), and the bark blast sends birds, cats, rats and cops on screen running.

Christmas & weather

The seasonal dressing. Snow caps and string lights are separate layers over the brownstones so they stay bright instead of being tinted brown; the late-night storm turns from rain to sleet to snow and leaves snow on the street.

Cast

The characters — every animation state, from playerFrames.js / dogFrames.js / drawMattTexture, playing. For Christmas, Nicole and Stella wear Santa hats (Stella also has a gold collar bell).
